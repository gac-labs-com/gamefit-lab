About GameFit Lab

GameFit Lab is an independent guide for people who spend a lot of time gaming and want to feel better doing it. We write about sleep, posture, eye strain and focus.

How we pick recommendations

We research each product or app, read the terms and prices, and compare it with alternatives. We tell you when we have not tested something ourselves. We only recommend what we would point a friend to.

We are not doctors. Nothing on this site is medical advice, and it is no substitute for seeing a professional. If you have pain, vision problems, sleep disorders or mental health concerns, see a qualified professional.
